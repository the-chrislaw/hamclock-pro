# Vendored planetary theory (VSOP87D, Earth)

- Data: `src/domain/astro_vsop/vsop87d_earth.inc`, generated by `src/domain/astro_vsop/gen_vsop87d.py` from `VSOP87D.ear` of P. Bretagnon and G. Francou, Astron. Astrophys. 202, 309 (1988), CDS catalogue VI/81 (https://cdsarc.cds.unistra.fr/ftp/VI/81/, fetched 2026-10-19).
- Licence: distributed by the CDS as open scientific data with citation requested; no restrictive licence stated. Numeric coefficients only.
- Truncation: terms with amplitude under 2e-7 (rad for L and B, AU for R) are dropped, leaving 156 terms (about 8 kB), roughly 0.05 arcsec in longitude.
- Evaluator: `src/domain/astro_vsop.{hpp,cpp}` (our own code).
- Validation: `widget-eclipse-vsop_regression` against Meeus ch. 25 example 25.b (longitude within 0.01 arcsec, radius within 1e-6 AU) and annual perihelion/aphelion bounds.
