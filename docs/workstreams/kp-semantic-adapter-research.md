## Evidence that can and cannot be promoted

**Estimated observation versus official/forecast — high confidence.** The [SWPC Planetary K-index product](https://www.spaceweather.gov/products/planetary-k-index) calls its chart an estimated three-hour index, links the observed JSON endpoint, states minute chart updates and identifies contributing observatories. The derived NOAA estimate is distinct from the official network's Kp. This supports naming the product **NOAA estimated Kp**, dimensionless, three-hour characterization. A minute chart refresh does not make each row an instantaneous magnetometer reading or guarantee a minute JSON regeneration cadence. The product page shows UTC context, but its page header time is not a row dictionary.

**Intervals and preliminary status — high confidence for physics; unresolved wire alignment.** [SWPC Station K and A Indices](https://www.spaceweather.gov/products/station-k-and-indices), Details, describes preliminary station K monitoring minute by minute, finalized station K after prescribed 0000–0300 through 2100–2400 intervals, near-real-time estimated Kp from a subset of the official stations, and end-of-period estimates. Station a is equivalent three-hour amplitude; daily A averages eight station a indices during a UT day. Planetary Ap analogously averages eight converted Kp amplitudes. Therefore Kp, station K, lowercase a, daily A and planetary Ap require separate types. This page does **not** define JSON `a_running` computation/window, `station_count` membership/coverage policy, or whether `time_tag` denotes bin start, bin end or estimate publication time. Do not substitute daily A for `a_running`, apply a station-dependent nT conversion, divide station_count by 8/13 into a confidence percentage, or mark an observed bin final solely because three hours elapsed.

**Current schema — high confidence.** [NWS SCN26-21](https://www.weather.gov/media/notification/pdf_2026/scn26-21_Data_Format_Changes_Impacting_SWPC_Products.pdf), page1, identifies observed and forecast Kp endpoints and moves header-row arrays to object entries with numeric values except time tags around March31,2026. It confirms syntax direction, not the field semantics above. Existing parser preserves raw civil tags, optional Kp/running-a/count, input order and duplicates; it performs no time-zone, interval, freshness or rights interpretation. Its immutable parse result is the correct offline boundary.

**Zone-less time tags — unresolved direct specification; medium confidence UTC inference.** NOAA's product/UT operational context supports UTC as an eventual adapter interpretation. The retrieved primary documents do not explicitly bind this endpoint's zone-less `time_tag` to UTC or define interval alignment. Searches scoped to official SWPC domains for these exact keys found no authoritative field dictionary. A guessed zone, local-computer conversion or a third-party application's appended Z is insufficient evidence. The attempted services text README retrieval failed; this is an access limitation, not proof no metadata exists. Preserve the raw tag and explicit `TimeSemantics::Unconfirmed` until either a product-specific authoritative statement or an independently reviewed, clearly labelled product-policy interpretation resolves the gate. Do not silently claim the timestamp was provider-specified UTC.

**Reuse/attribution — high confidence published policy, medium confidence this derived-product scope.** [NWS reuse policy](https://www.weather.gov/disclaimer), Use of NOAA/NWS Data and Products, permits lawful reuse of NWS information unless otherwise noted, forbids copyright/endorsement misrepresentation and modified material presented as official. Its third-party section directs users to providers for further-use rights. The Kp product identifies cooperative magnetometer contributors; that alone does not establish a separate restriction on NOAA's derived factual estimated index, nor clear redistribution of partners' raw measurements. Recommended limited scope: NOAA's published estimated-index numbers with source/product URL and received/raw-tag metadata, voluntary SWPC and contributor credit, no logo or endorsement, original synthetic test fixtures. Do not import raw partner datasets under this scope. A reviewer must inspect source-specific notices before distributing real captures; no blanket legal certification follows from a NOAA hostname.

**Forecast is separate — high confidence.** The [3-Day Geomagnetic Forecast product](https://www.spaceweather.gov/products/3-day-geomagnetic-forecast) distinguishes observed Ap, deterministic Ap predictions, probability categories and three-hour Kp predictions. Forecast issue time, valid interval and prediction type must remain separate; a future valid interval is legitimate forecast data, never a future observed reading. This product description is not an exact JSON forecast schema. Forecast syntax/null/status/UTC/bin dictionary needs its own research/parser contract before coding.

## Proposed exclusive coding tickets and failure behavior

These are replacement policies, not assertions about NOAA guarantees. Root retains all shared build/dashboard/settings ownership.

- **KS0 — API proposal only:** NEW `src/providers/kp_semantics.hpp`; distinguish raw civil tag, unconfirmed versus explicitly supplied time basis, receipt UTC and monotonic clock, replay activation time, interval interpretation, estimated/forecast/replay identity, optional metadata and revision/provenance. No source code until independently reviewed. Archive/source digest and parser provenance label must not be confused: a caller's label is not rights verification.
- **KR1 — pure offline replay adapter:** NEW `src/providers/kp_replay_adapter.hpp/.cpp`, `tests/kp_replay_adapter_regression.cpp`; injected replay civil/UTC mapping, bounded1024-row document, no file/network/system clock. Nullable Kp produces Missing, never zero. Keep null/zero running-a/count distinct as uninterpreted metadata; a zero count may be a proposed conservative unusable-data rule only if explicitly named policy, not provider semantics.
- **KR2 — chronological replay selection:** NEW `src/providers/kp_replay_timeline.hpp/.cpp`, `tests/kp_replay_timeline_regression.cpp`; stable chronological ordering with provenance positions; identical-time identical-data entries may coalesce under a named policy, conflicting duplicates return Ambiguous instead of choosing arbitrary last/first. Backwards input order is allowed structurally, not assumed newer; newer missing row must not expose an older reading as current. Whole-document rejection preserves previous cache with an explicit failure/stale state; successful empty input means no readings. Replay cannot accept a live provider identity from an arbitrary label.
- **KL1 — live semantic adapter, gated:** NEW `src/providers/noaa_kp_adapter.hpp/.cpp`, `tests/noaa_kp_adapter_regression.cpp`; resolve zone/bin contract first. Maintain received-age and source-period separately; repeated fetch of unchanged bytes cannot rejuvenate source observation age. Detect future observed tags, rollback and stale source while preserving historical series. Exact freshness threshold remains product policy after timestamp/bin interpretation, not three hours automatically. Publication receipt, interval end and wall-clock now must not be conflated. Never infer reliability from station_count alone or interpolate across nulls/outages.
- **KF0 — forecast research first:** NEW forecast contract document; future valid intervals, issue age, deterministic/probability separation, forecast-versus-observation labels, schema/version and bounds. Separate decoder after peer gate; no observed-to-forecast substitution on outage.

Acceptance: zero/missing distinction, leap day/day boundary, replay before/at/after activation, wrong basis/future/rollback, duplicate conflicts, backwards rows, empty/rejected documents, receipt refresh of unchanged observation, source labels/digests and overflow/caps. Scientific quality remains Estimated/Unconfirmed as appropriate; parser acceptance never automatically sets Valid. Source a/count metadata remain uninterpreted until authoritative semantics are pinned.
